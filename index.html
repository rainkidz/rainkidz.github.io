How to install APKs

Straightforward sideloading guide for Android 7.0 and above.

1

Download .apk

Tap the download button above or grab the asset from GitHub Releases.

2

Open Files

Tap the notification when finished or locate the file in your system Downloads folder.

3

Allow Source

Toggle "Install unknown apps" for your browser/file manager if requested by Android.

4

Launch & Enjoy

Tap Install. The application is now ready in your app drawer without extra accounts.

Note on Google Play Protect
Play Protect may show an "Unrecognized app" or "Blocked by Play Protect" prompt for direct sideloads. This is normal for apps distributed outside the Play Store because they are self-signed. You can safely choose "Install anyway" after verifying our published SHA-256 hash.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear technical answers on security, architecture, and licensing. View all →

Are these apps safe?

Yes. Every APK is built and released by rainkidz, hosted on GitHub Releases, and each app page shows a SHA-256 fingerprint so you can verify the file after downloading. Apps are free and contain ads (Google AdMob); no account is required.

Why does Play Protect warn during installation?

Play Protect warns about any app not installed through the Play Store. This is normal for sideloaded apps — review the SHA-256 fingerprint on the app page, then install anyway.

How do updates work?

Tubenime checks for new versions in Settings and notifies you; Toyota Parts Price updates automatically via the Play Store. For Wispmail, check the catalog for new releases.

Where are old versions?

Open an app's detail page — the "Version Archive" table lists releases with direct download links. Older builds are also on GitHub Releases.
