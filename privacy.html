Privacy Policy — Tubenime

Last updated: January 15, 2026 · Effective date: October 12, 2025 · Document ID: PDL-TN-2026-A

Summary in Plain English

Tubenime does not require accounts, does not collect personal identity data, and processes video URLs entirely locally on your device. The app is free and shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect your Advertising ID and approximate location under Google's own policies. No remote analytics, no crash reporting, no centralized user logs.

01Data We Collect

Tubenime is designed strictly around the principle of data minimization. The application operates solely as an on-device utility and media manifest parser.

Explicitly Handled Data:

  • Media Resource URLs: Web links shared via Android Intent or pasted by you into the interface (e.g., links from supported public video portals) strictly to fetch stream addresses.
  • Local UI Preferences: Dark mode preference, preferred container format (MP4/WEBM), and designated local download folder paths saved exclusively inside Android SharedPreferences.

Data We Never Collect:

  • Names, Emails, or Phone Numbers
  • Precise or Coarse GPS Location
  • Hardware Fingerprints or MAC

02How We Use Data

Input URLs are parsed dynamically in runtime memory to decode publicly available video stream endpoints and captions.

Local Parsing Only: The parsing engine operates directly within your client runtime. No intermediary proxy, scraping gateway, or telemetry aggregator receives the link. (Separately, the bundled Google AdMob SDK communicates with Google's ad servers as described below.)

No User Profiling: We maintain zero database records of queries, parsed links, or historical downloads. Your search or stream history is kept solely within your device's private SQLite sandbox.

Advertising: To keep the app free, Tubenime shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use your Advertising ID and approximate location to personalize ads, under Google's Privacy Policy. You can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

03Data Storage & Security

Tubenime has zero cloud infrastructure. We operate zero centralized servers, zero remote databases, and zero authentication clusters.

Physical File Storage

Media files, audio buffers, and temporary segments are written solely to directories chosen by you via Android Storage Access Framework (SAF) or scoped app storage.

Ephemeral Memory Scrubber

Stream parsing buffers and HTTP chunk headers are automatically purged upon download completion or app termination.

04Third-Party Services & Network Requests

When performing stream resolutions or downloads, Tubenime functions as a standard user-agent client establishing direct HTTP/HTTPS connections to media hosting providers.

Direct Target Platforms

Connections are routed directly to public media CDNs (such as YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, Threads, and Facebook) based on the URL provided. Those platforms may observe your IP address and standard HTTP request headers, subject to their respective terms and privacy policies.

Third-Party SDK Manifest

Our source code and binary release artifacts include:

• Google AdMob (play-services-ads) — advertising
• Google Play Billing — in-app purchases
• NO Firebase Analytics or Crashlytics
• NO Unity, AppLovin, Sentry, Adjust, or AppsFlyer SDKs

05Children's Privacy

Our software does not address or knowingly solicit information from children under 13 (or under 16 in certain EEA jurisdictions). Because Tubenime does not require account registration, collect personal profiles, or maintain server-side records, we do not store child-identifiable telemetry in any format.

06Changes to This Policy

We may periodically review and update this privacy statement to reflect improvements in on-device architecture, new supported platforms, or regulatory requirements.
